# Trading King Calculator

A professional, high-performance financial suite designed for traders who need quick and precise risk management tools. Built with a sleek monochrome dark glassmorphism UI for zero distractions and instant calculations.

---

## ⚡ Features Included

- **Profit / Loss Calculator:** Easily calculate net PnL, PnL percentage, and total exit value for both Long and Short positions with leverage scaling.
- **Stop Loss & Take Profit:** Determine exact price points and absolute dollar risk/reward before entering any trade.
- **Dynamic Average Down / Up (DCA):** Add multiple entry positions dynamically to calculate your true dollar-cost average entry price.
- **Position Size Calculator:** Calculate exact contract/lot sizing based on your portfolio account balance and risk tolerance.
- **Risk / Reward Ratio:** Evaluate your trade setup efficiency with real-time R:R status feedback.
- **Target Price Calculator:** Work backward to find the exact exit price required to hit your target profit percentage.
- **Break-Even Calculator:** Calculate your exact break-even exit price, accounting for maker/taker exchange fees.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5:** Clean structural layout.
- **Tailwind CSS (CDN):** Modern utility-first styling.
- **Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+):** Pure, zero-dependency logic for instant live calculation updates.
- **Inter Font:** Clean, readable modern typography.
